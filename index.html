About Huntsville

Huntsville, Alabama—known as the "Rocket City"—has transformed from its space exploration roots into one of America's most dynamic technology and innovation hubs. With NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center, Redstone Arsenal, and a booming tech corridor, the city attracts highly educated professionals and forward-thinking businesses.

The Huntsville metro area boasts one of the highest concentrations of engineers per capita in the nation, creating a unique marketing landscape where B2B, government contracting, and consumer markets intersect. Understanding this ecosystem is key to marketing success in our region.

Key Industries: Aerospace & Defense, Technology, Healthcare, Manufacturing, and Professional Services
