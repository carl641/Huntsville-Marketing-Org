Business Support Organizations

These organizations provide valuable resources, networking opportunities, and support for businesses looking to grow their marketing efforts in Huntsville.

Huntsville/Madison County Chamber of Commerce

The Chamber is the central hub for business networking in the Huntsville area. Membership provides access to networking events, business resources, and visibility in the local business community.

  • Networking Events: Monthly business after-hours, breakfast meetings, and industry-specific gatherings
  • Business Directory: Exposure to customers searching for local businesses
  • Committees: Marketing and communications committee for hands-on involvement
  • Advocacy: Business-friendly policy advocacy at local and state levels
  • Resources: Small business resources, relocation information, economic data

Location: 225 Church Street NW, Huntsville, AL 35801

The Catalyst Center

Huntsville's innovation and entrepreneurship hub, The Catalyst Center supports startups and growing companies with resources, mentorship, and community.

  • Startup Support: Programs and resources for new ventures
  • Events: Pitch competitions, workshops, and networking events
  • Mentorship: Access to experienced entrepreneurs and business leaders
  • Coworking: Flexible workspace and collaboration opportunities
  • Connections: Links to investors, partners, and customers

Location: 2225 Drake Avenue SW, Huntsville, AL 35805

Professional Groups

Connect with fellow marketing and advertising professionals through these industry organizations.

AAF-North Alabama (American Advertising Federation)

The local chapter of the American Advertising Federation brings together advertising, marketing, and creative professionals for networking, education, and recognition of great work.

  • ADDY Awards: Annual competition recognizing the best advertising work in North Alabama
  • Monthly Programs: Educational sessions featuring industry speakers and topics
  • Networking: Regular opportunities to connect with local creative professionals
  • Student Programs: Mentorship and scholarship opportunities for aspiring marketers
  • Industry Advocacy: Representing advertising industry interests

Membership: Open to advertising and marketing professionals, agencies, and students

Other Professional Organizations

Additional groups that support marketing and related professionals:

  • PRSA-North Alabama: Public relations professionals network and professional development
  • Women in Defense - Tennessee Valley: Networking for women in defense industry, including communications roles
  • AMA (American Marketing Association): National organization with virtual resources and events
  • Social Media Club: Periodic local meetups for social media professionals
  • Content Marketing Institute: Online resources and virtual community

Tip: Join organizations aligned with your specialty and actively participate to maximize value

Education & Learning

Develop your skills through formal education and professional development programs available in the Huntsville area.

UAH Small Business Development Center

The Alabama SBDC at UAH provides free consulting and low-cost training for small businesses, including marketing guidance.

  • One-on-one consulting
  • Marketing workshops
  • Business plan development
  • Market research assistance

Cost: Consulting is free; workshops have minimal fees

SCORE Huntsville

SCORE provides free mentoring from experienced business professionals, many with marketing expertise.

  • Free business mentoring
  • Marketing strategy guidance
  • Workshops and webinars
  • Resource library

Cost: Free mentoring and most resources

Local Colleges & Universities

Pursue formal education or professional certificates in marketing:

  • UAH: Marketing degree program and professional certificates
  • Alabama A&M: Business and marketing programs
  • Calhoun Community College: Business courses and certificates
  • Drake State: Business and technology programs

Tip: Many employers offer tuition reimbursement

Online Learning Resources

Supplement local education with online platforms:

  • Google Digital Garage: Free digital marketing certification
  • HubSpot Academy: Free inbound marketing and sales certifications
  • LinkedIn Learning: Extensive marketing course library (often free through local libraries)
  • Coursera & edX: University-level marketing courses
  • Semrush Academy: SEO and digital marketing certifications
